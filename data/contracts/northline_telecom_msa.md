# Master Services Agreement: Northline Telecom (illustrative)

Mock contract for the BillBridge prototype. Not a real agreement.
Client ID: NLT. Contractor: Meridian Engineering Services.

## 3. Billing Basis

### 3.1 Billable Units
Each completed pole attachment design is one billable unit. A unit is complete when it is
recorded as completed in the engineering workspace for the billing period.

### 3.2 Standard Effort
The standard engineering effort is 8.0 hours per pole attachment unit, covering project
management, engineering design, and QC review. Billable hours are reconciled against
completed units at this standard each billing period.

### 3.3 Hourly Billing
Hours are billable only when logged against a valid project number in the time-tracking
system during the billing period.

## 4. Variances and Adjustments

### 4.1 Variance Tolerance
Where recorded hours per unit are within 15% of the standard effort, hours are billed as
recorded. Where recorded hours exceed the standard by more than 15% but not more than 50%,
billable hours are capped at the standard effort plus 15% (9.2 hours per unit). Hours above
the cap are non-billable unless covered by an approved change order.

### 4.2 Complex Make-Ready Work
Where field conditions require make-ready engineering beyond standard scope (for example,
pole replacement, crowded communication space, or utility-requested redesign), hours may
be billed above the standard up to a cap of 10.0 hours per unit. Billing above the standard
under this section requires supervisor approval and a documented justification. Hours above
the 10.0 hours-per-unit cap are absorbed by the Contractor unless an approved change order
states otherwise.

### 4.3 Under-Run
Where recorded hours are below the standard effort, the Contractor bills recorded hours.
Where recorded hours are more than 50% below the standard, the Client may request an audit
of the completed units before payment.

### 4.4 Change Orders
Work outside the scope of this agreement is billable only under a written change order
signed by both parties before the work is invoiced.

## 5. Invoicing

### 5.1 Invoice Timing
The Contractor invoices monthly after billing period close. Disputed line items must be
raised within 30 days of the invoice date.
